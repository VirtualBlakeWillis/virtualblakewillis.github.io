# The AI selects Bullets; it never writes resume text

The Resume Tailor's audience is people deciding whether to hire Blake, so every word on a Tailored Resume must be one Blake wrote. AI is used only to read the Job Input (the Job Analysis) and to choose among pre-written Bullets (Selection Rounds); it never rewords a Bullet or drafts a new one. The Job Analysis paragraph itself is never shown to visitors — only its Matched Tags are.

## Considered Options

- **Light rephrasing of chosen Bullets to echo the job's wording**: rejected; a hiring reader can't tell which claims are Blake's, and output stops being reproducible.
- **Generated Bullets**: rejected for the same reason, plus fabrication risk.

## Consequences

Tailoring quality is capped by the Bullet Library. Gaps are fixed by writing more Bullets, not by prompting.
